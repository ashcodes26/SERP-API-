# Career Plan reference assets

Inter 400, 500 and 600 are bundled locally for the My Career Plan and shared sign-in/student signup presentations. No app-wide font or theme is overridden. Both supplied Stitch exports specify Inter.

- Source: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap (the stylesheet named in the supplied Stitch My Career Plan HTML; TrueType files served by fonts.gstatic.com).
- Copyright and redistribution terms: accompanying `OFL.txt`, obtained from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsms/inter/master/LICENSE.txt.
- Loaded with the existing Expo Font package; no new dependency or font build plugin.

The matching logo at `../../images/career-plan-logo.png` is the original 80 × 80 PNG referenced by the user-supplied Stitch export, downloaded unchanged from its `img` source on lh3.googleusercontent.com. It is displayed at 32 × 32 in the Career Plan shell. Other screens retain their existing brand treatment.
