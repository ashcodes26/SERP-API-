## Assessment and evidence rules

SkillUp has a working application worth refining: real account/scope enforcement, persisted student and faculty workflows, deterministic analysis, bounded provider adapters, consented resume revision, and substantial API/service tests. The next work should close specific workflow, privacy, and release gaps while preserving those foundations.

Evidence labels below:

- **V**: reproduced in this audit using a browser or isolated synthetic API/service probe.
- **T**: exercised by the existing automated tests, rerun successfully here.
- **S**: verified directly in current source; not necessarily exercised through a browser.
- **H**: hypothesis or operational exposure requiring further validation.

Feature status **verified working** is restricted to the stated tested boundary. It does not imply live-provider, native-device, or production acceptance. **Partially implemented** identifies a real missing interaction or deployment requirement. **Not verified** is not equivalent to broken.

Priorities: **P0** security/data-loss/release blocker; **P1** critical workflow failure; **P2** major UX/reliability/maintainability issue; **P3** minor polish. A conditional P0 deployment gate is explicitly distinguished from an active exploit. Implementation risk means risk of making the proposed change, not finding severity.
